#+TITLE: 05 — web-app login (hierarchical statechart)
#+STARTUP: showall
* Difficulty
4 / 8 — a containment poset (tree) with cyclic navigation.
* Provenance
learntla.com, /State Machines/ (hierarchical section).
* The machine
- Leaves: =LogOut=, =Main=, =Settings=, =Report1=, =Report2=.
- Hierarchy: =LogIn ⊃ {Main, Settings, Reports}=, =Reports ⊃ {Report1, Report2}=.
- Navigation (cyclic): =Main↔Settings=, =Report1↔Report2=, =LogIn→LogOut=,
  =LogOut→Main=, =Reports→Main=.
- Structure S: the *containment hierarchy is a tree — a genuine poset* (distinct
  from the linear chain); the transition graph on top of it has cycles.
* What to implement
Fold a navigation log into the current leaf; the projection may keep a *flag per
ancestor* — and here the down-set characterization DOES hold on the tree: a
node's flag implies its parent's (ancestor-closure). Completeness needs
=TopDown[state] = {}= — exactly one leaf.
* The lesson
Down-sets generalize from a chain to any poset: valid ancestor-sets are order
ideals of the tree. Soundness holds (tree = DAG); completeness needs the
one-leaf rule (spec §5 on a tree).
